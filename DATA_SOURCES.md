# Data source

[IBM employee attrition sample](https://github.com/IBM/employee-attrition-aif360/blob/master/data/emp_attrition.csv). The download script obtains the sample from the publisher repository. `outputs/metrics.json` pins the exact evaluated CSV with a SHA-256 checksum.

This is a published sample, not customer or employee records from Yousuf Patel’s employment. MIT covers this repository’s authored code, not third-party data. Consult the publisher’s repository and dataset terms before redistribution.
